Questions, answered

Straight from the 2027/28 packages. Anything else, ask us directly.

What exactly does SKOLAR help with?

Four stages of your application to study in Italy: admission and university application, pre-enrolment and document legalisation, scholarship assistance, and student visa assistance. The full list for each stage is above.

How much does it cost?

Each stage is ₹30,000. Complete application support — all four stages — is ₹1,10,000 for the 2027/28 academic year.

MEA apostille is included for 2 educational documents; each additional page is ₹1,500.

What’s different between the Bachelor’s and Master’s packages?

The stages, inclusions and price are the same. The Bachelor’s admission stage adds TOLC / SAT guidance, and its visa stage covers post-arrival guidance as well as pre-departure. The document checklists differ — see the checklist.

How many universities can I apply to?

Up to 5 shortlisted universities, as part of the admission stage.

Do you help with IELTS?

The admission stage includes guidance on English language requirements (IELTS / MOI / TOEFL). 1-to-1 IELTS coaching is available at a discounted price with the complete package.

Which scholarships do you help with?

Regional scholarships (DSU, ERSU, DISCo Lazio, ERGO) and merit scholarships (MAECI, Invest Your Talent in Italy).

SKOLAR also prepares the supporting documentation — family composition certificate, property certificate and financial assets — with 2 certified Italian translations and ISEE Parificato guidance.

Do I need to visit any offices in person?

Yes. You’ll visit the HRD office and VFS yourself for HRD attestation, the visa and DOV — these steps have to be done physically by the applicant.

Do you arrange accommodation?

The visa stage includes accommodation arrangement guidance, for visa purposes only.

Who decides on admission, scholarships and the visa?

Universities decide admissions, scholarship bodies decide awards, and visas are decided by the Italian consulate. SKOLAR’s role is to guide and prepare you at each stage.

Are fees refundable?

Fees paid are subject to a no-refund policy.

How do I send my documents?

Email them to applicationdocument2728@gmail.com with the subject line “Full Name | Bachelor’s Admission 2027–2028” (or Master’s). The checklist can fill in the subject for you.
