Questions, answered

How much does it cost?

₹30,000 per stage, or ₹1,10,000 for all four (2027/28). Extra apostille pages are ₹1,500 each.

Bachelor’s or Master’s — what changes?

Same stages, same price. Bachelor’s adds TOLC / SAT and post-arrival guidance, and the document checklists differ.

Do you help with IELTS?

Yes — IELTS / MOI / TOEFL guidance, plus discounted 1-to-1 IELTS coaching with the complete package.

Do I need to visit any offices myself?

Yes. HRD attestation, DOV and the visa need you at the HRD office and VFS in person.

Who makes the final decisions?

Universities decide admission, scholarship bodies decide awards and the Italian consulate decides visas. SKOLAR guides and prepares you.

Are fees refundable?

No. Fees paid are subject to a no-refund policy.
